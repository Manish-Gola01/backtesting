# Live Trading Readiness & Current Proof of Profitability

This document defines the quantitative evaluation framework used by the platform to assess whether a strategy is ready for live capital deployment, and the empirical boundaries regarding proof of profitability.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: Backtest vs. Live Reality

A profitable backtest is **necessary but never sufficient** for live deployment.

| Dimension | Backtest Simulation | Live Trading Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **Fills & Execution** | Modelled fills at next open / limit price | Order queuing, partial fills, slippage variability |
| **Market Impact** | Zero impact assumed | Larger sizes move order books against the strategy |
| **Latency** | Instant execution at bar boundary | Network ping, exchange gateway latency, race conditions |
| **Market Regimes** | Fixed historical sample | Non-stationary distribution shifts, regime changes |
| **Liquidity** | Constant bid/ask availability | Flash crashes, illiquidity spikes, spread widening |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A profitable backtest does NOT constitute mathematical or legal proof of future live profitability.**
> Historical performance only establishes *empirical evidence of edge under modelled conditions*. Transition to live trading requires progressive stages of verification.

---

## 2. Live Trading Readiness Rubric

The platform executes a multi-dimensional readiness evaluation across 8 empirical checks:

### 1. Statistical Sufficiency (`sample_size`)
- **Threshold**: Requires at least 30 closed trades ($\ge 30$).
- **Rationale**: Small samples are dominated by random walk noise. Any metric computed with fewer than 30 trades suffers from high standard error.

### 2. In-Sample Net Profitability (`profitable_in_sample`)
- **Threshold**: Net profit after all simulated costs (brokerage, slippage, exchange fees) must be strictly $> 0$.
- **Rationale**: A strategy that cannot beat costs in historical data will lose money faster in live trading.

### 3. Positive Mathematical Expectancy (`positive_expectancy`)
- **Threshold**: Average net P&L per trade must be $> 0$.
- **Rationale**: Expectancy $E = (P_{\text{win}} \times W_{\text{avg}}) - (P_{\text{loss}} \times L_{\text{avg}}) - \text{Costs}$. A positive expected value is required for capital growth.

### 4. P&L Consistency & Non-Concentration (`consistency`)
- **Threshold**: Largest single winning trade must account for less than 50% of total net profit.
- **Rationale**: Prevents strategies where an overall profit was generated by a single lucky tail event while the rest of the trades incurred net decay.

### 5. Drawdown Containment (`drawdown`)
- **Threshold**: Maximum peak-to-trough equity drawdown must be $\le 25\%$ (or within configured risk limits).
- **Rationale**: Excessive drawdown leads to margin exhaustion, trader psychology breakdown, and live failure.

### 6. Consecutive Loss Streak Survival (`loss_streak`)
- **Threshold**: Maximum consecutive losing trades must be $\le 8$.
- **Rationale**: Accounts must have enough risk buffer to survive inevitable losing runs without catastrophic equity destruction.

### 7. Out-of-Sample / Walk-Forward Robustness (`out_of_sample`)
- **Threshold**: Net profit must remain positive on held-out out-of-sample data, and in-sample to out-of-sample degradation must be monitored.
- **Rationale**: Guards against parameter overfitting and data snooping.

### 8. Current Proof of Profitability Assessment (`current_proof_of_profitability`)
- **Assessment**: Evaluates whether current results demonstrate empirical edge under backtesting, and flags that live proof requires forward paper-trading validation.

---

## 3. Readiness Verdicts & Action Gates

| Verdict | Meaning | Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| `RESEARCH-PROMISING` | Passed critical gates ($\ge 70\%$ score, valid OOS, sufficient sample). | Proceed to real-time paper trading on live feeds. |
| `NEEDS MORE VALIDATION` | Positive edge observed, but sample size, consistency, or drawdown requires further validation. | Expand historical dataset or refine parameters. |
| `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` | In-sample positive, but Out-of-Sample (OOS) testing has not yet been executed. | Run walk-forward / held-out test before drawing conclusions. |
| `NOT READY` | Negative net profit, negative expectancy, or critical risk boundaries breached. | Do not trade live. Reject or overhaul strategy hypothesis. |

---

## 4. Current Proof of Profitability Framework

When reviewing any backtest, the platform reports the **Current Proof of Profitability** status:

- **Status `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE_ONLY`**:
  - The strategy has proven empirical profitability over the historical sample.
  - This is **not** guaranteed future proof.
  - Live execution variance, slippage, and queue delays remain unvalidated.
- **Status `NO_EDGE_DEMONSTRATED`**:
  - Net P&L is negative or expectancy is non-positive.
  - Strategy has no evidence of profitability in the tested market/timeframe.

### The 4-Stage Deployment Pipeline

1. **Stage 1: Deterministic Backtest** (Look-ahead bias free, realistic slippage & fee model).
2. **Stage 2: Walk-Forward & Stress Testing** (OOS validation, parameter sensitivity tests).
3. **Stage 3: Forward Paper Trading** (Minimum 30–60 days on live market tick feed, monitoring execution slippage vs model).
4. **Stage 4: Micro-Lot Live Deployment** (Minimum capital allocation with circuit breakers).
